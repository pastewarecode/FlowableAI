FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

Our pricing depends on the scope and complexity of the systems we're implementing. We confirm exact pricing after the discovery call, once we understand your workflows, tools, and goals.

Pricing includes a one-time setup fee, with a monthly retainer fee to maintain and monitor the system.

If there isn't a clear return on investment, we won't recommend moving forward.

Do I need to change the tools I already use?

Usually not. We build around your existing phone number, calendar, CRM, and spreadsheets wherever possible.

What happens if the AI can't answer a customer?

It hands the conversation to you. Anything unusual, urgent, or sensitive is passed to a real person, so customers are never left stuck.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. We handle the setup and train your team. Day to day, you just see booked jobs and organized leads.

How is my customer data handled?

Each system only accesses the data it needs to run, and we never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do you only work with businesses in Calgary?

We're based in Calgary, Alberta, and work with businesses across Canada and the United States.

What should I prepare before the session?

Before the session, it helps to:

  • Have a general overview of your current processes and tools
  • Know where things feel manual, slow, or disorganized
  • Be clear on your goals (saving time, increasing conversions, scaling, etc.)

No technical prep is required — we'll guide the conversation and ask the right questions.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes — you can reschedule your appointment using the link included in your booking confirmation email.

We ask that any changes be made at least 24 hours in advance so we can ensure smooth scheduling for all clients.
